# Summit Series — Participant Screening Criteria

**Document type:** screening_criteria
**Fabricated:** This organization and these policies are invented test material for the PISA prototype. Not a real organization, not real policy, not clinical or legal advice.
**Version:** 2.3 — reviewed by program medical advisor
**Instruction to screeners:** These criteria exist because the program combines a 48-hour fast, sustained exertion at altitude, cold immersion, and intensive psychological work, all at a site 90 minutes from emergency care. Criteria marked EXCLUSIONARY mean the applicant should not be accepted under the standard program; a criterion may only be set aside with written documentation resolving the specific risk, reviewed by the program medical advisor. Criteria marked CAUTION require follow-up and resolution before clearance.

## A. Exclusionary criteria (hard)

- **A1. Lithium therapy.** Any applicant currently prescribed lithium. Rationale: the 48-hour fast plus exertion and altitude create a significant dehydration risk; lithium has a narrow therapeutic window and dehydration can precipitate toxicity. No on-site capability to monitor levels.
- **A2. Psychotic disorder, or any psychotic episode, at any time.** Includes schizophrenia spectrum, schizoaffective disorder, or any break from reality. Rationale: fasting, sleep disruption at altitude, isolation from supports, and emotionally intensive group work are destabilizing stressors.
- **A3. Bipolar I disorder, or any manic or hypomanic episode within the past 5 years.** Rationale: sleep disruption and fasting are recognized triggers for mood episodes; the program cannot manage an emergent episode.
- **A4. Unstable cardiovascular disease.** Includes: heart attack within 12 months; unstable angina; symptomatic or undiagnosed arrhythmia / irregular heart rate; uncontrolled hypertension (untreated, or above 140/90 despite treatment); history of stroke within 12 months. Rationale: altitude exertion and cold immersion both impose acute cardiovascular load.
- **A5. Seizure history, unless seizure-free and off any restrictions for 2+ years with documentation.** Rationale: fasting and sleep disruption lower seizure threshold; remote setting.
- **A6. Pregnancy or possible pregnancy.** Rationale: fasting, altitude, and cold immersion are not appropriate.
- **A7. Insulin-dependent diabetes.** Rationale: a 48-hour fast with exertion cannot be safely managed without clinical monitoring. (Non-insulin diabetes: see C4.)
- **A8. History of an eating disorder (anorexia, bulimia, binge eating disorder, or other disordered eating), at any time.** Rationale: a structured 48-hour fast is contraindicated for anyone with an eating disorder history regardless of current status; the fasting protocol may not be modified per-participant.
- **A9. Suicidal ideation, intent, or attempt within the past 12 months; or self-harm within the past 12 months.** Rationale: the program is emotionally destabilizing by design and removes the participant from their support structures.
- **A10. Current untreated substance use disorder, or physiological alcohol dependence.** Rationale: the program requires abrupt full abstinence in a remote setting; unmanaged withdrawal is a medical emergency.

## B. Caution criteria — mental and psychological (follow-up required before clearance)

- **B1. Trauma history without current therapeutic support.** Applicants disclosing significant trauma who are not currently working with a therapist or counselor require follow-up: the life-review and group-process components can surface trauma material, and integration support afterward is expected. Resolution: applicant establishes or documents a current therapeutic relationship, or the intake conversation establishes adequate support.
- **B2. Dissociative symptoms.** Reports of depersonalization, derealization, significant memory gaps, or "checking out" require follow-up on frequency, severity, and triggers. Escalate to exclusionary if episodes are frequent, uncontrolled, or triggered by stress or fasting.
- **B3. Bipolar II or cyclothymia, or hypomanic episode more than 5 years ago.** Requires prescriber letter confirming stability and that the fast and sleep disruption are acceptable.
- **B4. Major depressive disorder, currently treated and stable.** Requires confirmation of stability (no episode requiring treatment change in 6 months) and prescriber awareness of the caffeine cessation and fasting protocol.
- **B5. Panic disorder or severe anxiety.** Follow up on triggers, frequency, and management, with specific attention to cold immersion and the 8-hour solo period.
- **B6. Any deferred, skipped, or "let's discuss in person" answer in the mental health, trauma, or substance use sections.** Always follow up in the intake conversation; never treat as a negative answer.
- **B7. Recent major loss or life crisis (bereavement, divorce, job loss within 6 months).** Follow up on stability and timing; the program's own materials state participants must be able to "handle some instability."

## C. Caution criteria — medical (follow-up required before clearance)

- **C1. Controlled hypertension on medication.** Requires written physician clearance specifically addressing: exertion at 8,000–10,000 ft, cold-water immersion, and a 48-hour fast while continuing the medication. Note: diuretics (e.g., hydrochlorothiazide) compound fast-related dehydration and require explicit physician comment.
- **C2. Beta-blockers (e.g., metoprolol, propranolol, atenolol).** These blunt heart-rate response and can mask overexertion cues at altitude and in cold water. Requires prescriber clearance for the specific activities.
- **C3. SSRI/SNRI antidepressants.** Generally compatible, but require: confirmation the applicant will NOT alter dosing for the retreat, prescriber awareness of the 48-hour fast, and screening for any concurrent St. John's Wort or other serotonergic supplement use (see C6).
- **C4. Non-insulin diabetes or prediabetes.** Requires physician clearance specifically for the 48-hour fast, and a written plan for glucose self-monitoring during the fast.
- **C5. Asthma.** Requires: current controller/rescue inhaler plan, inhaler carried on all hikes, and follow-up on whether cold air or cold water has previously triggered symptoms.
- **C6. Interaction-prone supplements.** St. John's Wort (interacts with many prescription medications and adds serotonergic load with SSRIs/SNRIs), regular grapefruit consumption (inhibits metabolism of many medications, including some blood pressure medications), and passionflower (sedative load). Follow up on any of these when the applicant also takes prescription medication.
- **C7. Anticoagulants / blood thinners.** Follow up: fall risk on trail and remoteness from care.
- **C8. Baseline fitness concern.** Any "no" or qualified answer to the groceries-upstairs question, or self-reported inability to sustain moderate exercise, requires follow-up and possibly a graded conditioning plan before arrival.
- **C9. Medications requiring food.** Any medication that must be taken with food requires a prescriber plan for the 48-hour fast.
- **C10. High caffeine dependence (≥4 caffeinated drinks/day).** Not disqualifying, but follow up on the pre-arrival taper plan; abrupt cessation causes withdrawal headaches that compound altitude effects.

## D. Substance use

- **D1. Heavy alcohol use (≥15 drinks/week or any daily use):** follow up per A10 for dependence indicators; if dependence is suspected, exclusionary.
- **D2. Cannabis use ≥4 times/week:** follow up on cessation plan and sleep impact (cannabis cessation commonly disrupts sleep, compounding altitude sleep disruption).
- **D3. Any use of non-prescribed stimulants, opioids, or sedatives within 3 months:** follow up; escalate per A10 as indicated.

## E. Positive indicators (record as green flags)

- **E1.** Current, ongoing therapeutic relationship (therapist, counselor, or structured peer support).
- **E2.** Prior successful experience with prolonged fasting, altitude exertion, or cold exposure.
- **E3.** Strong, specifically named support network for the post-program period.
- **E4.** Physician clearance already obtained and provided.
- **E5.** Realistic, self-aware statement of goals and concerns (as opposed to idealized expectations of transformation).

## F. Cross-section checks (screeners: look for these explicitly)

- **F1.** Self-described health rating inconsistent with checked conditions or listed medications.
- **F2.** A medication listed in the medication table whose indication implies a condition not disclosed in the medical or mental health sections (or vice versa).
- **F3.** Substance quantities in the consumption table inconsistent with the narrative substance-use answers.
- **F4.** Goals or expectations that indicate the applicant believes the program will resolve a clinical condition (the program is not treatment and must not be positioned as such).
