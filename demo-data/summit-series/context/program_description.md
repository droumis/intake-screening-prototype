# Summit Series: Executive Renewal Retreat — Program Description

## Overview

The Summit Series is a seven-day residential retreat for senior professionals, held at a mountain lodge at 7,800 ft elevation, approximately 90 minutes from the nearest hospital. The program combines sustained physical challenge with intensive psychological and interpersonal work. Cell coverage at the lodge is intermittent. Participants live on-site for the full duration.

## Daily Physical Demands

- **Altitude hiking (days 2, 3, 6):** Guided hikes of 6–10 miles with 1,500–2,500 ft of elevation gain, at altitudes between 7,800 and 10,200 ft. Pace is moderate but sustained; participants carry a 15 lb daypack. There is no vehicle support on trail.
- **Cold-water immersion (days 3 and 5):** Supervised immersion in an alpine lake or cold plunge tub, water temperature 45–55°F, for durations building from 90 seconds to 5 minutes.
- **Morning movement (daily):** 45 minutes of vigorous calisthenics or trail running.

## Dietary Protocol

- **Days 1–2 and 5–7:** A structured whole-food menu, lower in sodium and free of alcohol and caffeine.
- **Days 3–4: a supervised 48-hour water-and-electrolyte fast.** Participants consume water, electrolyte solution, and herbal tea only. The fast overlaps with a day-3 hike (shortened route) and the day-3 cold immersion. Staff monitor participants, but this is a wellness program, not a medical facility; no clinical monitoring (labs, telemetry) is available on-site.
- Caffeine and alcohol are prohibited for the full seven days. Participants are asked to taper caffeine in the week before arrival.

## Psychological and Interpersonal Intensity

- **Daily group process sessions (2–3 hours):** Facilitated group work involving direct interpersonal feedback, disclosure of personal material, and structured vulnerability exercises. Sessions can surface strong emotions including grief, shame, and anger.
- **Solo reflection period (day 4):** 8 hours of unaccompanied silent reflection (within lodge grounds) during the second day of the fast, journaling only, no devices.
- **Life-review exercise (day 5):** A structured written and spoken exercise revisiting formative life experiences, including difficult ones, shared in small groups.

Participants routinely describe the week as emotionally destabilizing in the short term. The program is designed for psychologically stable adults who can tolerate temporary distress, sleep in shared quarters, and function with reduced privacy for seven days.

## Environment and Logistics

- Shared rooms (2 per room), communal bathrooms.
- 7,800 ft base elevation; participants arriving from sea level should expect altitude effects (headache, sleep disruption) in the first 48 hours.
- Nearest emergency department: 90 minutes by road. A staff member with wilderness first responder certification is on-site at all times.
- Participants may not bring alcohol, cannabis, or non-prescribed drugs. Prescribed medications are permitted and must be disclosed in advance.
- Devices are collected on arrival and returned at departure; participants may schedule one check-in call mid-week.

## What Participation Requires

A participant must be able to: complete sustained moderate-intensity hiking at altitude; tolerate a 48-hour fast concurrent with physical activity; tolerate cold-water immersion; engage in emotionally intensive group work without access to their usual support structures; and go seven days without caffeine, alcohol, cannabis, or contact with home except one scheduled call.
