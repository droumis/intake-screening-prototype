# Alder Creek Psilocybin Services — Program Description (Oregon)

**Document type:** program_description
**Fabricated:** Invented center and policies for the PISA prototype. The rule paraphrases below were written for this project, are not accurate legal summaries, and are not maintained against amendments. Not legal advice.

## Overview

Alder Creek is a licensed psilocybin service center operating under ORS 475A and OAR 333-333 (Oregon Psilocybin Services, Oregon Health Authority). Clients complete a preparation session with a licensed facilitator, an administration session at the licensed service center, and are offered an integration session, with facilitator follow-up within 72 hours of administration.

## The Administration Session

- Clients consume a psilocybin product **only** at the licensed service center, during the administration session, under continuous supervision of a licensed facilitator. Maximum total consumption per session is set by OPS rule (up to 50 mg psilocybin analyte, including any secondary dose agreed in writing beforehand).
- Minimum session duration scales with dose (up to six hours at the higher range); clients must remain on-site for the full minimum duration and may not leave during the session.
- Sessions typically involve reclining with eyeshades and music, with periods of intense emotion, memory, and altered sense of self and time. Challenging passages are common and are supported, not suppressed.
- Clients may not be visibly intoxicated at any session (preparation, administration, or integration) and no intoxicants other than the psilocybin product may be consumed on premises.
- Clients must arrange transportation home; driving after the session is prohibited until the following day.

## What the State Requires Before Administration

- Clients must be **21 or older** (ID checked on entry).
- Clients must complete at least one **preparation session** with the facilitator.
- Clients must review and complete the **OHA Client Information Form** with the facilitator before any administration session. The facilitator must evaluate the answers per OAR 333-333-5050(3); some answers legally preclude participation and others trigger required plans (see the screening criteria document).
- Where a client answer triggers a plan (medication administration, medical devices, mobility/emergency exit, interpreter, client support person, AAC/assistive listening), the plan must be written and in place before the session.

## What Participation Demands of a Client

A sustained non-ordinary state lasting several hours with no option to end it early; transient physiological effects (elevated heart rate and blood pressure, early nausea); several hours without food; separation from usual supports and devices during the session; and readiness to engage integration support afterward.

## Facility

Licensed premises with a designated administration area and client restroom; facilitators maintain the safety and support planning, transportation planning, and emergency protocols required by rule. Nearest emergency department: 25 minutes.
