# Reference Material — Oregon Regulatory Context

**Document type:** reference_material
**Fabricated:** Written for the PISA prototype as illustrative background. Not verified clinical or legal reference material.
**Purpose:** Screening-level background and citations for the Oregon dataset. No dosing guidance; medication decisions belong to prescribers.

## Primary sources

- **ORS 475A** — Oregon Psilocybin Services Act (Measure 109, 2020).
- **OAR 333-333** — Oregon Health Authority administrative rules (Oregon Secretary of State, division 333-333); amendments effective 1/1/2026 under Oregon Laws 2025 ch. 147.
- **OHA Client Information Form** (updated 1/1/26) — the mandatory pre-administration form; its Facilitator Review and Evaluation section (per OAR 333-333-5050(3)) is the source of every R-A and R-B disposition in the screening criteria.
- **Oregon Psilocybin Services (OPS)** program pages, oregon.gov/psilocybin.

## Key regulatory facts for screeners

- **Three legal exclusions only:** lithium in the last 30 days; current thoughts of harming self or others; lifetime diagnosis/treatment of active psychosis. Everything else on the state form routes to consultation encouragement or written plans. House criteria intentionally go further; flags must keep the two bases distinct.
- **Pregnancy and mushroom allergy are not legal exclusions** in Oregon (informed-risk discussion and alternative-product encouragement, respectively). A screening system that excludes for these is applying house policy, not law.
- **Session mechanics:** administration only at licensed service centers; 21+; preparation session required; up to 50 mg psilocybin analyte per session; minimum durations scale with dose (to six hours at the top range); facilitator ratios apply to group administration sessions (1:25 below 5 mg down to 1:2 at 35–50 mg; 25-client cap) — operational requirements, not participant-eligibility screening.
- **Dual licensure (from 1/1/2026):** facilitators also licensed by one of seven named Oregon boards may provide health/behavioral health services during preparation and integration sessions and must notify clients when doing so. Screening relevance: routing and documentation, not eligibility.
- **Visible intoxication** at any session precludes services; no other intoxicants on premises.

## Clinical background (house-criteria basis)

- Lithium + classical psychedelics: case literature associates the combination with seizures and severe adverse events — the basis of the state's only medication exclusion and our H-series conservatism.
- MAOIs: interaction risk; discontinuation is prescriber-managed and out of scope — house exclusion H-1.
- Psychosis/bipolar spectrum: recurrence and episode-trigger risk under psilocybin, sleep disruption, and emotional intensity — basis of R-A3 and H-3/H-10/H-11.
- Cardiovascular: transient HR/BP elevation during sessions — basis of H-4/H-12.
- SSRIs/SNRIs: commonly attenuated response; never advise dose changes — prescriber territory (H-6).
- Serotonergic supplements (St. John's Wort, passionflower) and grapefruit: interaction and load considerations; discontinuation windows set at intake per consulting clinician.
