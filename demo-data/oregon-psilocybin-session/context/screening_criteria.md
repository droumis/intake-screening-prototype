# Alder Creek — Client Screening Criteria (Oregon)

**Document type:** screening_criteria
**Fabricated:** Invented center and policies for the PISA prototype. The rule paraphrases below were written for this project, are not accurate legal summaries, and are not maintained against amendments. Not legal advice.
**Version:** OR-1.0 — aligned to the OHA Client Information Form (updated 1/1/26) and OAR 333-333-5050(3)
**Structure:** Part 1 is the **regulatory floor** — Oregon law's required questions and required dispositions, cited to the rule. Part 2 contains **house criteria** — this center's clinical standards, which are deliberately stricter than the legal floor. Flags must cite the criterion that produced them so the reviewer can distinguish "the law requires this outcome" from "our policy requires this follow-up." Where a house criterion is stricter than the regulatory floor, that is intentional and is not a document conflict.
**Ground rules:** Screeners never advise a client to alter, taper, skip, or stop any medication; medication questions are referred to the client's prescriber, and prescriber-directed plans must be documented. Screening outcomes are recommendations to the human reviewer; the facilitator makes the final eligibility evaluation required by rule.

## Part 1 — Regulatory floor (OAR 333-333; Client Information Form)

Every client must complete the OHA Client Information Form with the facilitator before an administration session. The intake packet includes its questions verbatim in the "Oregon Client Information Form" section. Dispositions below are what the rule requires of the facilitator for each "yes."

### R-A. Participation precluded by rule (EXCLUSIONARY — cannot be resolved by follow-up)

- **R-A1. Lithium within the last 30 days** (Form Q 3(a)). A "yes" means the client **may not participate** in an administration session. Note the 30-day window: recent discontinuation still inside 30 days is a "yes."
- **R-A2. Current thoughts of causing harm, or wanting to cause harm, to self or others** (Form Q 3(h)). A "yes" means the client **may not participate**. Screening should also route the person to appropriate support resources.
- **R-A3. Ever diagnosed with or treated for active psychosis** (Form Q 3(j)). Lifetime question; a "yes" means the client **may not participate**, regardless of how the client characterizes the episode (including as a spiritual experience).

### R-B. Required dispositions (NOT exclusionary — a "yes" triggers the action below)

- **R-B1. Currently under treatment by a medical, clinical, or other healthcare provider** (Q 3(b)) → facilitator **shall encourage the client to consult that provider** about the risks of consuming psilocybin. Resolution: consultation encouraged and documented.
- **R-B2. Prior allergic reaction to mushrooms or other fungi** (Q 3(c)) → client **should be encouraged toward an alternative psilocybin product** (not whole or homogenized fungi). This is NOT an exclusion under Oregon rule. Resolution: product-type plan noted.
- **R-B3. Medications or supplements that may need to be consumed during the session** (Q 3(d)) → encourage scheduling to avoid mid-session dosing where possible; encourage pharmacist/provider consultation on contraindications; if mid-session administration is needed and the client cannot self-administer, a **client support person must be identified**. Resolution: written medication plan.
- **R-B4. Interpreter needed** (Q 3(e)) → identify an interpreter present in person or virtually. Logistics.
- **R-B5. Catheter/ostomy/toileting, ambulation/transfer, or medical device assistance** (Q 3(f)) → **written assistance or medical device plan**, identifying a client support person where the client cannot manage alone.
- **R-B6. AAC or assistive listening device support** (Q 3(g)) → identify an appropriate client support person.
- **R-B7. History (not current) of causing or wanting to cause harm to self or others** (Q 3(i)) → facilitator **shall encourage consultation** with a medical or clinical provider about risks. Not exclusionary by rule; see house criterion H-2 for this center's stricter handling.
- **R-B8. Pregnant or feeding with breast milk** (Q 3(k)) → facilitator **must inform the client that risks are unknown**. This is NOT an exclusion under Oregon rule. Resolution: informed-risk discussion documented.
- **R-B9. Assistive mobility devices** (Q 3(l)) → **written emergency exit plan** for the service center.
- **R-B10. Assistance to consume psilocybin products** (Q 3(m)) → identify a client support person.
- **R-B11. Other conditions, sensitivities, or health concerns shared** (Q 3(n)) → **written accommodation plan** describing reasonable steps (e.g., infection-control measures for immunocompromise).

### R-C. Other regulatory requirements

- **R-C1. Age 21+** — under 21 cannot receive services.
- **R-C2. Visible intoxication** at any session precludes services that day.
- **R-C3. Preparation session** completed before administration.
- **R-C4. Transportation plan** — no driving until the following day.

## Part 2 — House criteria (this center's standards; stricter than the legal floor)

### H-Red (house-exclusionary absent extraordinary documentation)

- **H-1. MAOI antidepressants.** Not addressed by Oregon rule; excluded by house policy for interaction risk. Any resolution requires prescriber documentation reviewed by our consulting clinician.
- **H-2. Suicidal ideation, intent, attempt, or self-harm within the past 12 months.** The rule excludes only *current* thoughts (R-A2) and encourages consultation for history (R-B7); house policy treats the last 12 months as exclusionary pending consulting-clinician review.
- **H-3. Bipolar I, any manic episode, or hypomanic episode within 5 years.** Not addressed by Oregon's form at all; house-exclusionary for episode-trigger risk.
- **H-4. Unstable cardiovascular disease** (heart attack/stroke within 12 months, unstable angina, symptomatic or undiagnosed arrhythmia, uncontrolled hypertension). Psilocybin transiently raises heart rate and blood pressure.
- **H-5. Current untreated substance use disorder or physiological alcohol/sedative dependence.**

### H-Caution (follow-up before clearance)

- **H-6. SSRIs/SNRIs** — confirm no self-directed dose changes; prescriber aware; screen for serotonergic supplements (St. John's Wort, passionflower — discontinuation plan per intake).
- **H-7. Seizure history** without 2+ years seizure-free documentation.
- **H-8. Trauma history without current therapeutic support** — establish support or a concrete integration plan.
- **H-9. Dissociative symptoms** — frequency/severity/triggers; escalate if frequent or uncontrolled.
- **H-10. Hypomanic episode more than 5 years ago, bipolar II/cyclothymia** — prescriber letter + consulting-clinician review.
- **H-11. First-degree relative with a psychotic or bipolar I disorder** — consulting-clinician review.
- **H-12. Controlled hypertension on medication** — prescriber clearance addressing transient HR/BP rise; grapefruit interaction check.
- **H-13. Deferred, skipped, or "let's discuss in person" answers** in mental health, trauma, sexuality, or substance sections — always follow up; never treat as "no." Sexuality-section deferrals handled one-on-one.
- **H-14. Difficult prior psychedelic experience** — follow up on support, resolution, learnings; escalate to R-A3 if the account contains psychotic features.
- **H-15. Cannabis ≥4×/week or heavy alcohol use** — cessation planning; dependence check per H-5.
- **H-16. Recent major loss or crisis (within 6 months)** — timing and stability conversation.

### H-Green (positive indicators)

- **H-G1.** Current therapeutic relationship or integration appointment scheduled. **H-G2.** Prior well-supported, well-integrated psychedelic experience. **H-G3.** Established contemplative practice. **H-G4.** Named, aware support network. **H-G5.** Realistic goals and self-aware fears. **H-G6.** Provider consultation already completed (satisfies R-B1's encouragement in advance).

### Cross-section checks

- **X-1.** Self-described health inconsistent with medications, checked conditions, or hospitalizations. **X-2.** A medication implying an undisclosed condition (or vice versa) — including medications implying lithium-adjacent mood treatment. **X-3.** Consumption-table quantities inconsistent with narrative answers. **X-4.** Goals framing the session as treatment or cure for a clinical condition — expectation reset required; escalate if stability depends on the outcome. **X-5.** Prose accounts containing unrecognized R-A3 features (a "break," "the veil lifting," hospitalization) narrated neutrally — evaluate what is described, not the client's label. **X-6.** Date arithmetic on windows: lithium 30 days (R-A1), suicidality 12 months (H-2), mania 5 years (H-3) — compute from dates given, don't take characterizations like "a while ago" at face value.
