# Juniper Ridge Group Psilocybin Retreat — Program Description

**Document type:** program_description
**Fabricated:** This organization and these policies are invented test material for the PISA prototype. Not a real organization, not real policy, not clinical or legal advice.

## Overview

Juniper Ridge is a licensed psilocybin service center offering a three-day group retreat under state psilocybin services regulations. Groups of six clients work with two certified facilitators. The retreat takes place at a rural property approximately 40 minutes from the nearest emergency department. Clients stay on-site for all three days.

## Structure

- **Day 1 — Preparation.** Group orientation, individual check-ins with a facilitator, review of intentions, safety agreements, and the group container. Light shared meals. Clients surrender vehicles keys and devices for the duration (one scheduled check-in call available).
- **Day 2 — Administration session.** Clients consume a psilocybin product in a group ceremony setting per the service center's license and state administration rules. The session lasts a minimum of 6 hours and commonly 7–8. Clients spend most of the session lying down with eyeshades and a curated music program, with facilitators present throughout. Clients eat a light early breakfast and then do not eat until the session concludes. Clients may not leave the premises during the session and may not drive until the following day.
- **Day 3 — Integration.** Group integration circle, individual facilitator check-ins, rest, and departure planning. Clients are strongly encouraged to have an integration appointment (therapist, counselor, or structured peer support) scheduled within two weeks of the retreat.

## What the Experience Demands

- **A sustained non-ordinary state of consciousness lasting many hours**, which can include intense emotions (grief, fear, awe, joy), vivid imagery, re-experiencing of memories including difficult ones, altered sense of self and time, and periods of confusion or disorientation. Challenging passages are common and are worked with, not prevented.
- **Physiological effects.** Psilocybin transiently raises heart rate and blood pressure, and commonly causes nausea early in the session. Clients remain semi-fasted through the session.
- **A group container.** Clients share the ceremony space with five others. Sounds of others' emotional processes (crying, movement, vocalizing) are part of the environment. Group sharing on days 1 and 3 involves personal disclosure.
- **Reduced access to usual supports.** No devices during the retreat; one scheduled call. Clients sleep on-site in shared cabins (2 per cabin).
- **Abstinence requirements.** No alcohol for 3 days prior and during; no cannabis or other non-prescribed psychoactive substances during, with a pre-retreat cessation plan agreed at intake; caffeine limited to one morning serving.
- **Medication ground rule.** Clients must not alter, skip, or taper any prescribed medication for this retreat except under the explicit direction of their own prescriber. Facilitators never direct medication changes; all medication questions are referred to the client's prescriber, and any prescriber-directed plan must be documented before the administration session.

## Environment and Logistics

- Rural setting; nearest emergency department 40 minutes by road. Facilitators maintain current first aid/CPR certification and follow the center's emergency protocols.
- The administration room is a single large space; bathroom access requires walking a short distance with facilitator support available.
- Clients must arrange their own transportation home on day 3 or be picked up; driving on day 2 is prohibited.

## What Participation Requires

A client must be able to: tolerate a many-hour non-ordinary state without the option to end it early; remain in a shared space with other clients in process; manage the session's transient cardiovascular load; go without food through the session and without their usual substances and devices for three days; and engage integration support afterward.
