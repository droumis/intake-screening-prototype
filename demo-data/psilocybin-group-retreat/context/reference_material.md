# Reference Material: Psilocybin Screening — Interaction and Risk Notes

**Document type:** reference_material
**Fabricated:** Written for the PISA prototype as illustrative background. Not verified clinical or legal reference material.
**Purpose:** Curated background for the screening assistant, at screening level only. This document informs *why* criteria exist and what follow-up questions matter. It contains no administration or dosing guidance; all medication decisions belong to the client's prescriber and the consulting clinician.

## Medications

| Medication / class | Screening-relevant concern | Criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Lithium | Case literature associates lithium + classical psychedelics with seizures and severe adverse events | A1 (exclusionary) |
| MAOIs (phenelzine, tranylcypromine) | Pharmacological interaction; unpredictable intensity; discontinuation is a prescriber-managed clinical matter, out of scope | A2 (exclusionary) |
| SSRIs/SNRIs | Commonly associated with attenuated subjective response; serotonergic load considerations; never advise dose changes — prescriber territory | C1 |
| Tricyclics | Interaction reports; clinician review | C2 |
| Blood-pressure medications | Session transiently raises HR/BP; clearance should address that specifically; several agents (e.g., some calcium channel blockers) interact with grapefruit | C3, C5 |
| Tramadol | Lowers seizure threshold; serotonergic | C6 |
| Benzodiazepines / sedative-hypnotics | Dependence assessment; session-day plan via prescriber | C7, A9 |
| Diabetes medications | Semi-fasted session day requires a prescriber plan; session-day medication/device access must be recorded | C8 |
| "Take with food" medications | Session is many hours and semi-fasted; prescriber plan | C10 |

## Supplements

- **St. John's Wort:** serotonergic and a broad enzyme inducer; the intake form asks about it by name. Discontinuation window set at intake per consulting clinician; combined with SSRIs it raises serotonergic load.
- **Passionflower:** mild MAO-related activity and sedative effect; the form asks about it by name for this reason. Regular use → intake plan.
- **Grapefruit:** inhibits metabolism of many drugs (notably some blood-pressure and psychiatric medications). Relevant only when prescription medication is present; agree an abstention window.

## Physiology and setting

- **Cardiovascular:** transient elevation of heart rate and blood pressure during sessions is well documented; this is the basis of A6 and C3. The center is 40 minutes from emergency care, which lowers the threshold for caution.
- **Nausea** early in session is common; relevant for clients with GI conditions or emetophobia (note at intake).
- **Seizure threshold:** basis of A7 and C6 caution.

## Psychological screening

- **Psychosis history (A3):** classical psychedelics can precipitate recurrence in vulnerable individuals; personal history is treated as exclusionary regardless of how the client frames the episode. Screeners should recognize reframed narratives: "spiritual emergency," "kundalini episode," "downloads," "the veil lifted for a week" accompanied by functional collapse, hospitalization, or others' alarm.
- **Bipolar spectrum (A4/B4):** episode-triggering risk via the session itself and disrupted sleep/routine.
- **Recent suicidality (A5):** sessions surface difficult material by design; the setting is non-clinical.
- **Trauma (B2):** vivid re-experiencing is common; support before and after is the mitigation, not avoidance of the material.
- **Dissociation (B3):** a many-hour non-ordinary state can intensify dissociative patterns; frequency and controllability are the key follow-up axes.
- **Group format considerations:** clients affected by others' emotional expression (sounds of crying, movement) should be prepared for it; severe social anxiety or panic (B6) warrants specific discussion of the shared space and no-early-exit structure.
- **Expectation setting (F4):** clients framing the retreat as a cure for depression or another condition need a reset conversation; unrealistic expectation is a known contributor to difficult experiences and poor integration.

## Substance cessation

- **Alcohol:** 3-day pre-retreat abstinence per program rules; daily drinkers need a dependence check first (A9/D1).
- **Cannabis:** regular users (≥4x/week) commonly report sleep disruption and vivid dreams for 1–2 weeks after stopping; agree a cessation timeline at intake (D2).
- **Caffeine:** limited to one morning serving on-site; heavy users should taper beforehand to avoid withdrawal headaches on session day.
