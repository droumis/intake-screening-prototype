To reduce intake friction for the summer cohorts:

1. **SSRI clients.** Facilitators may suggest that clients consider holding their morning SSRI dose on the administration day to improve session response. (Conflicts with the medication ground rule and C1, which prohibit facilitators from directing any medication change and require all such plans to come from the client's prescriber.)

2. **Remote hypomanic history.** A single hypomanic episode more than 3 years ago may be treated as a caution item resolvable at intake rather than requiring the 5-year exclusion window. (Conflicts with A4.)

All other v1.4 criteria remain in force.
