# Bluebird Healing Center — Participant Screening Criteria (Colorado)

**Document type:** screening_criteria
**Fabricated:** Invented center and policies for the PISA prototype. The rule paraphrases below were written for this project, are not accurate legal summaries, and are not maintained against amendments. Not legal advice.
**Version:** CO-1.0 — aligned to 4 CCR 755-1 (DORA Office of Natural Medicine Licensure), Rule 2.2 scope-of-practice and Rule 2.5 safety-screen requirements, and 1 CCR 212-3 (DOR Natural Medicine Division)
**Structure:** Part 1 is the **regulatory framework** — Colorado defines **no categorical participant exclusions**; identified risk factors instead trigger clearance pathways and documentation requirements. A criterion in Part 1 must therefore never produce an unresolvable exclusionary flag: the correct output is a serious, unresolved flag whose resolution criteria are the documented pathway. Part 2 contains **house criteria** — this center's clinical standards, which may be stricter than the regulatory framework (a facilitator or healing center may always decline to provide services); house-based declinations must cite H- criteria, never the rule. Flags must carry the criterion ID so the reviewer can distinguish "the law requires this documentation" from "our policy requires this outcome."
**Ground rules:** Screeners never advise a participant to alter, taper, skip, or stop any medication; medication questions are referred to the participant's prescriber or the clearance pathway. Screening outcomes are recommendations to the human reviewer; the facilitator's licensed scope of practice determines what the facilitator may do.

## Part 1 — Regulatory framework (4 CCR 755-1)

### R-1. Safety screen (Rule 2.5(A)(2))
Every prospective participant completes a written safety screen covering **physical health conditions, mental health conditions, and medication risk factors**. The intake packet includes it as the "Colorado Safety Screen" section; the participant attests in writing to the accuracy and completeness of all health information provided.

### R-2. Risk factors → clearance pathways (Rule 2.2)
If the safety screen identifies a risk factor, the facilitator **may not independently provide services** to that participant. Services may proceed only when at least one of the following is documented (**RED until documented; resolvable; never `hard_flag`)**:

- **R-2a.** A **referral** for natural medicine services from a licensed medical or behavioral health provider (MD/DO, APN, PA, or a clinical facilitator);
- **R-2b.** **Medical clearance** from the participant's treating medical or behavioral health provider; or
- **R-2c.** A documented **consultation and risk review** with a medical or behavioral health provider licensed to diagnose and treat the identified condition (Colorado or the participant's home state).

If the consultation identifies **heightened risk**, the participant and facilitator must additionally develop a **written safety plan**, and the participant must provide written informed consent to proceed (**R-2d**).

- **R-3. Lithium or antipsychotic medications — strictest tier.** A participant taking lithium or an antipsychotic requires the R-2 pathway in all cases; even a Clinical Facilitator may not proceed independently unless prescribing those medications is within their own secondary scope. Flag level: **red, resolvable only by documented R-2 clearance/consultation plus, given known interaction risk, an R-2d safety plan.** Not an exclusion in law — but never a yellow.
- **R-4. Other psychotropic medications** identified on the screen (e.g., SSRIs/SNRIs, mood stabilizers other than lithium, stimulants, sedatives): the facilitator must provide a **written recommendation** that the participant obtain applicable medical/behavioral consultation (Rule 2.2(A)(9) framework). Flag level: yellow; resolution: written recommendation documented, and any obtained consultation recorded.
- **R-5. Facilitator-type routing.** The screen's output includes which facilitator type fits the participant: participants seeking treatment of a physical or behavioral health condition, or presenting active diagnoses, are appropriate for a **Clinical Facilitator** (who may act within their secondary license's scope) or require the R-2 pathway with a standard Facilitator. Screening flags should state the routing implication where relevant.
- **R-6. Attestations and consents:** accuracy of health information; informed consent to risks and circumstances of services; parameters of any physical-touch consent. Missing or declined attestations block scheduling (data-quality/logistics flag).
- **R-7. Age 21+.**
- **R-8. In-session duties relevant to screening:** the facilitator continuously assesses distress and suicidality during administration — screening should surface anything that sharpens that in-session vigilance (history of SI, panic, dissociation) even where no pathway is triggered.

## Part 2 — House criteria (this center's standards)

### H-Red (house policy: decline pending consulting-clinician review, even though rule permits a pathway)

- **H-1. Current thoughts of harming self or others.** Colorado law does not categorically exclude; house policy declines and routes to crisis-appropriate care.
- **H-2. Suicidal ideation, intent, attempt, or self-harm within 12 months.** House-declined pending consulting-clinician review; if review supports proceeding, R-2c/R-2d documentation is additionally required.
- **H-3. Personal psychosis history at any time**, however framed by the participant (including as spiritual emergency). House-declined pending consulting-clinician review.
- **H-4. Bipolar I or any manic episode; hypomanic episode within 5 years.** House-declined pending consulting-clinician review + prescriber input.
- **H-5. MAOI antidepressants.** House-declined; any exception requires prescriber documentation reviewed by the consulting clinician.
- **H-6. Unstable cardiovascular disease** (MI/stroke within 12 months, unstable angina, symptomatic/undiagnosed arrhythmia, uncontrolled hypertension).
- **H-7. Current untreated substance use disorder or physiological alcohol/sedative dependence.**

### H-Caution (follow-up before clearance; use the R-2 pathway where a listed risk factor is present)

- **H-8. Controlled hypertension on medication** — risk factor: route through R-2 with clearance addressing transient HR/BP elevation; check grapefruit interactions.
- **H-9. SSRIs/SNRIs** — R-4 written recommendation; confirm no self-directed dose changes; screen serotonergic supplements (St. John's Wort, passionflower — discontinuation plan per intake).
- **H-10. Seizure history** without 2+ years documentation — risk factor: R-2 pathway.
- **H-11. Pregnancy or possible pregnancy** — risk factor: R-2 pathway with obstetric consultation; house guidance: strongly discourage.
- **H-12. Mushroom/fungi allergy** — risk factor: allergist input via R-2c; product-form discussion.
- **H-13. Trauma history without current therapeutic support** — establish support or concrete integration plan.
- **H-14. Dissociative symptoms** — frequency/severity/triggers; informs R-8 vigilance; escalate if uncontrolled.
- **H-15. First-degree relative with psychotic or bipolar I disorder** — consulting-clinician review.
- **H-16. Deferred, skipped, or "let's discuss in person" answers** in mental health, trauma, sexuality, or substance sections — always follow up; sexuality-section deferrals one-on-one.
- **H-17. Difficult prior psychedelic experience** — support, resolution, learnings; escalate to H-3 if psychotic features.
- **H-18. Cannabis ≥4×/week or heavy alcohol use** — cessation plan; dependence check per H-7.
- **H-19. Recent major loss or crisis (6 months)** — timing and stability conversation.

### H-Green (positive indicators)

**H-G1** current therapeutic relationship or integration appointment booked · **H-G2** prior well-supported, integrated psychedelic experience · **H-G3** established contemplative practice · **H-G4** named, aware support network · **H-G5** realistic goals and self-aware fears · **H-G6** clearance/consultation documentation already in hand (pre-satisfies R-2).

### Cross-section checks

**X-1** health self-rating vs meds/conditions/hospitalizations · **X-2** medication implying an undisclosed condition (or vice versa) · **X-3** consumption table vs narrative · **X-4** treatment/cure expectation framing (note: participants MAY lawfully seek treatment via a Clinical Facilitator — X-4 here means checking that expectation matches the facilitator type per R-5, not suppressing the goal) · **X-5** neutral narration of psychotic-feature events · **X-6** window arithmetic (12 months, 5 years) from dates given.
