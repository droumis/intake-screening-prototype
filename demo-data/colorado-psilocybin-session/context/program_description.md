# Bluebird Healing Center — Program Description (Colorado)

**Document type:** program_description
**Fabricated:** Invented center and policies for the PISA prototype. The rule paraphrases below were written for this project, are not accurate legal summaries, and are not maintained against amendments. Not legal advice.

## Overview

Bluebird is a licensed healing center operating under Colorado's Natural Medicine Health Act (C.R.S. 12-170 / SB23-290), DORA's Office of Natural Medicine Licensure rules (4 CCR 755-1), and the Department of Revenue Natural Medicine Division rules (1 CCR 212-3). Participants complete an initial consultation with safety screening, a preparation session, an administration session at the healing center, and integration session(s).

## The Administration Session

- Regulated natural medicine is consumed only in the licensed administration area, observed by the facilitator. Servings are limited to 10 mg total psilocin each; the participant and facilitator agree the number of servings during preparation — there is no fixed per-session cap in rule, so the agreed plan governs.
- Minimum session durations scale with total dose (from one hour at the lowest tier to five hours at the upper range). Participants remain on-site for the full duration; sessions involve reclining with eyeshades and music, intense emotion, and altered sense of self and time; challenging passages are supported, not suppressed.
- During the session the facilitator monitors participant wellbeing continuously, including ongoing assessment for distress and suicidality, per rule.
- Only participants, facilitators, and authorized licensees may be present in the administration area; any other person requires each participant's prior written consent.
- Supportive touch, if any, is limited to hands, feet, or shoulders and only under a physical-touch agreement consented to in advance; consent is revocable at any time.
- Participants arrange safe transportation home; driving after the session is prohibited.

## What the State Requires Before Administration

- Participants must be **21 or older**.
- An **initial consultation** and a written **safety screen** covering physical health, mental health, and medication risk factors (the intake packet includes the center's safety-screen section).
- Written **participant attestations**: accuracy and completeness of health information provided, informed consent to risks, and the parameters of any consent to physical touch.
- Where the safety screen identifies **risk factors**, Colorado's rules define clearance pathways rather than exclusions — see the screening criteria document. Required referrals, clearances, consultations, and any safety plan must be documented before the administration session.
- A **preparation session** before administration.

## What Participation Demands of a Participant

A sustained non-ordinary state lasting several hours with no early exit; transient physiological effects (elevated heart rate and blood pressure, early nausea); several hours without food; separation from usual supports and devices during the session; and engagement with integration support afterward.

## Facility

Licensed healing center premises with a designated administration area; facilitator maintains emergency protocols, documentation, and record-keeping per rule. Nearest emergency department: 20 minutes.
